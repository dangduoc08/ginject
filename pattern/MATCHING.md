Patterns and topics are dot-separated segments. The syntax is glob-style (D-030): `*` is exactly one segment, and a trailing `**` is one or more. The broker's topics (R-2) and the logger's `MaskFields` use the same syntax.

## 9. Literals

`*` inside a segment, and `**` anywhere but last, are plain characters.
