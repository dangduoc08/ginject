## 3. Priority: exact > param > `*` > glob

When several children could match at the same position, the exact segment wins, then the param, then `*`, then globs.

## 7. A wildcard above the dead end wins

If the greedy walk already has a wildcard fallback, `Find` does not backtrack. That keeps params consistent with the returned route.

Registered routes: `/a/*/`, `/a/b/x/`, `/a/$/c/`

| Request path | Result | Params | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/a/b/c/` | ✅ `/a/*/` (wildcard fallback) | — | the wildcard `/a/*/`, not `/a/$/c/` |
| `/a/z/c/` | ✅ `/a/$/c/` (route) | `z` | does not enter `b`, so `/a/$/c/` matches |

## 8. Params stay with their route on wildcard fallback

The params returned always belong to the route the router will use.
